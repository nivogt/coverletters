## About the Job

The role is part of Google Cloud's Global Solutions and Consumer AI organization. The team aims to accelerate customer value and Google Cloud adoption by delivering innovative, repeatable, enterprise-ready solutions focused on business value and industry relevance.

As a Financial Services Solution Architect in the Innovation Delivery team, the hire will collaborate with clients to turn initial concepts into solutions. The work focuses on blockchain, tokenization, and AI-driven automation to modernize workflows for a strategic capital-markets customer, including Financial Market Infrastructures (FMIs) and high-stakes clearing, settlement, and custody environments.

The role combines problem solving, innovation, and building. Working with senior team members, the architect will design customer solutions and functional prototypes, including a scalable blockchain position-keeping service for tokenized instruments and a WalletNext service that allows AI agents to query real-time position data across Google Cloud clients.

Google Cloud is described as providing enterprise-grade technology and tools to organizations in more than 200 countries and territories to support digital transformation and critical business needs.

## Minimum Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
- 7 years of experience in software or data engineering, including programming in Python, Go, or Java, and experience with design patterns, testing frameworks, and API contract design.
- Experience in back-office clearance, trade reconciliation, and identifying or mitigating transaction settlement risks.
- Experience designing and implementing Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT) solutions and smart contracts within regulated Financial Market Infrastructures (FMIs), including managing the lifecycle of tokenized assets.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience using AI tools to significantly increase personal coding velocity and code quality.
- Experience in financial services, with familiarity with the regulatory and operational demands of clearing, settlement, or custody.
- Proficiency in BigQuery, Vertex AI, Dataflow, and Pub/Sub.
- Financial services regulatory knowledge covering data residency, encryption at rest and in transit (CMEK), and explainable AI requirements in banking.
- Strong understanding of relational, NoSQL, and analytical data modeling, such as Star Schema and Data Vault.
- Ability to independently drive discovery, turning an ambiguous business problem into a structured Product Requirements Document (PRD) and working technical demo.

## Responsibilities

- Design and architect a scalable position-keeping service for blockchain networks, supporting internal and external tokenized, detokenized, and hybrid financial instruments for next-generation financial architecture.
- Architect and oversee development of a wallet service that allows AI agents to query customers' real-time position data.
- Partner with client leads and business users to create PRDs, define Critical User Journeys (CUJs), and agree on success metrics.
- Evaluate and integrate modern data foundations, including relational databases, data lakehouses, and real-time streaming architectures; optimize pipelines for AI retrieval, agentic workflows, and real-time financial insights.
- Create clear technical guides to support a smooth handoff from proof of concept to engineering teams.

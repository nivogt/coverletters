# Role Summary: Core Competencies and Responsibilities

**Position:** Solutions Architect, Digital Platforms, Google Cloud  
**Focus:** Financial Services / capital markets / Financial Market Infrastructure  
**Primary location choices include:** Zürich, Switzerland

For a staged learning roadmap and conditional resume guidance, see [learning-plan.md](learning-plan.md).

## Core Competencies

1. **Capital-markets operations:** clearing, trade reconciliation, settlement-risk management, and ideally custody operations in regulated FMIs.
2. **DLT and tokenization:** practical architecture and implementation of distributed-ledger systems, smart contracts, and tokenized-asset lifecycle management.
3. **Software and data engineering:** substantial hands-on Python, Go, or Java experience; design patterns; testing frameworks; API contracts; scalable service design.
4. **Google Cloud data and AI:** BigQuery, Vertex AI, Dataflow, Pub/Sub, AI-assisted engineering, and data pipelines for retrieval and agentic workloads.
5. **Financial-services controls:** data residency, encryption in transit and at rest including CMEK, and explainable AI expectations in banking.
6. **Data architecture:** relational, NoSQL, and analytical models such as Star Schema and Data Vault, plus lakehouse and streaming patterns.
7. **Customer discovery and delivery:** turn ambiguous needs into PRDs, Critical User Journeys, success metrics, prototypes, and engineering-ready handoffs.
8. **Solution architecture and influence:** balance customer value, security, scalability, operational risk, and implementation trade-offs while collaborating with business users and engineering teams.

## Main Responsibilities

- Architect a scalable blockchain position-keeping service for tokenized, detokenized, and hybrid instruments.
- Lead architecture for a wallet service that exposes real-time customer positions to AI agents.
- Discover requirements with business stakeholders and define PRDs, user journeys, and measurable outcomes.
- Select and integrate databases, lakehouses, streaming, and AI-retrieval data foundations.
- Prototype the solution and document a clear transition path into production engineering.

## What a Strong Application Needs to Demonstrate

- Direct evidence for the explicit minimums, especially FMI clearance/settlement and regulated DLT/smart-contract delivery.
- Concrete engineering examples with languages, APIs, automated testing, systems designed, and personal contribution.
- Production-level knowledge of GCP services and financial-services data/security constraints.
- Customer-facing discovery: the problem, PRD or equivalent, prototype, success measure, and outcome.
- Specific results such as latency, throughput, reconciliation time, operational risk, cost, adoption, or delivery-speed improvement.
